Nicole Neumann and Sofía Zámolo Attend an Exclusive Fashion Show in Buenos Aires

17 October 2026 · 16:00 · Argentina

Designer Mirta Armesto brought together friends, clients and entertainment personalities for an exclusive presentation that combined fashion, social gatherings and a distinguished guest list.

L’AVOIR Editorial Desk · Editorial direction: Iliana Manzini

Buenos Aires was the setting for an exclusive fashion show that welcomed friends, clients and prominent figures from the entertainment industry. Among the guests were renowned models and television presenters Nicole Neumann and Sofía Zámolo, who attended the presentation and joined an event dedicated to fashion.

The gathering brought together personalities from different fields for an event that highlighted design and the experience of sharing a fashion presentation in an intimate and select setting.

With a guest list combining loyal clients, friends and public figures, the show offered an opportunity to bring the designer’s vision closer to her community and reaffirm the connection between fashion, identity and social life.

Editorial note: This report is based on the event information provided to L’AVOIR. Additional details about the collection were not included in the supplied material.
