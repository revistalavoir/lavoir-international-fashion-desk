Editorial identity

L’AVOIR is an independent editorial publication. Its International Fashion Desk covers fashion houses, Fashion Weeks, design, photography, cinema, sustainability and the business of fashion. The publication is directed from Puerto Madryn, Patagonia, Argentina, with an ambition to expand its coverage across Argentina, Latin America and international markets.

Editorial direction

Iliana Manzini

Founder and editorial director of L’AVOIR. Her editorial work connects fashion, culture and creative industries with an international perspective from Argentina.
